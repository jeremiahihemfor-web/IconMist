Minimal and clean, similar in feel to the current shop:
- Warm cream background, black text, small Kente accents (gold #D4A017, green, red) used sparingly:
  thin Kente stripe dividers, gold buttons/links.
- Bold, confident sans-serif headings; simple body font. No clutter, plenty of space.
- Homepage: big hero with the product photo of her in the umbrella and one clear "Shop AfroDry" button;
  a short "The Problem vs The Solution" row (splash vs dry); product feature row; FAQ; footer.
- Header/footer: "AfroDry" wordmark, minimal nav.
